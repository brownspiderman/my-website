Hailing from Amritsar, Punjab Himi is an innovative musician.

He has independently produced and released songs on BandCamp that have been recognised worldwide. He went to study music production at Musician's Institute, LA in 2020. After returning to india he applied what he learned in his hometown. He built a recording studio out in the garden, often staying there at night to work on projects.

Himi has written many songs and some have been successful. In 2025 he built a room for recording, and dubbing. He also built an app that plays custom music. Himi's music is available for purchase on BandCamp. He is currently building an indepedent music library that can be used in Ads and also works as a session musician and recording engineer.
