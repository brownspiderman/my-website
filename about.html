I'm a multimedia creator from Amritsar, Punjab.

I've pursued a degree in media communications from Drexel University, Philadelphia where I discovered music production and started learning how to put together songs when I was not working. I have continued releasing music independently on BandCamp. I went to study music production at Musician's Institute, LA in 2020 for a semester and returned to India to start my own studio.

In the years since I have continued to practice and put my own spin on things on songs, words, visuals. This website is a collection of some of those things that I've made over the years. It contains the DNA of what once was and is my ultimate reference for where this project will go in the future.

This website emerged as a multimedia project which took about a weekend to make but years to come together. Best, Himi
